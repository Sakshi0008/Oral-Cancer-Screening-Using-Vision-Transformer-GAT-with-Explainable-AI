Oral Health & Lesion Awareness Guide

Educational resource on common mucosal lesions, risk factors, and clinical referral protocols.

🩺

Warning Signs of Oral Lesions

Be vigilant for persistent red patches (erythroplakia), white plaques (leukoplakia), unhealed ulcers lasting >14 days, indurated mucosal swelling, or unexplained oral numbness.

🚬

Primary Etiological Risk Factors

Chronic tobacco consumption (smoked and smokeless/areca nut), frequent heavy alcohol intake, Human Papillomavirus (HPV-16/18), and persistent mechanical trauma to the mucosa.

🔬

Role of AI vs Clinical Biopsy

Artificial Intelligence models are triaging screening aids designed to flag suspicious patterns. They can never prove malignancy or cellular dysplasia. Tissue biopsy remains the sole definitive gold standard.

Hybrid Architecture Specifications

Exact architectural configuration of the 2,238,406 parameter ViT-GATv2 model.

Vision Transformer Backbone

  • Input: 224x224 RGB image
  • Patch Size: 16x16 (196 spatial patches)
  • Embedding Dim: 192 (Depth: 4 layers, 3 heads)
  • Parameters: 1,965,890 (Trained from scratch)
  • Checkpoint: models/vit_scratch_best.pth

Graph Attention (GATv2) Backbone

  • Nodes: 196 patch tokens (Dim: 192)
  • Adjacency: 4-neighbor spatial + Top-8 cosine similarity
  • Layers: 2 GATv2 layers (4 heads, hidden dim: 48)
  • Parameters: 222,722
  • Checkpoint: models/graph_hybrid_gatv2_best.pth

Multimodal Concatenation Fusion

  • Input: Concatenated ViT + GAT [192 + 192 = 384]
  • Classifier: Linear(384, 128) → LayerNorm → ReLU → Linear(128, 2)
  • Parameters: 49,794
  • Checkpoint: models/hybrid_vit_gat_concat_best.pth
  • Total Parameters: 2,238,406
